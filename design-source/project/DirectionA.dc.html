How dinner works

Seafood, the Alexandrian way.

    Pick your fish

    Choose from the day’s catch at the counter. It’s weighed by the pound.

  2. ٢02

    Tell the chef how

    Grilled, fried, baked, singari, rada, butterflied or deboned.

  3. ٣03

    Take a seat

    We seat you while your fish hits the fire.

  4. ٤04

    Add your sides

    Rice, tahini, babaganoush, Hamido salad and fresh pita.
